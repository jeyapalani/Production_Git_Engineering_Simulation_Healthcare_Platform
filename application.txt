Healthcare Platform
********************
Patient Management
- Patient registration is available.
- Patient demographic information can be maintained.

Appointment Management
- Patients can request appointments.
- Appointment details can be recorded.

Clinical Access
- Authorized clinical users can access patient information.
- Access to patient information is controlled by authorization rules.

Patient Registration
- Patients can register with the healthcare platform.
- Patient registration information is validated before being stored.
--------------------------------------------------------------------- B

Appointment Scheduling
- Patients can request appointments with healthcare providers.
- Appointment requests contain provider and appointment time information.
--------------------------------------------------------------------- C

Appointment History
- Patients can view previous appointments.
- Completed appointment details can be reviewed.
--------------------------------------------------------------------- D

Clinical Access
- Authorized clinical users can access required patient information.
- Access is restricted according to assigned permissions.
--------------------------------------------------------------------- E

Audit Management
- Patient information access can be recorded.
- Administrative actions can be reviewed for audit purposes.
--------------------------------------------------------------------- F

Appointment Notifications
- Patients can receive appointment notifications.
- Notification status can be recorded.
--------------------------------------------------------------------- G

Appointment Status
- Appointments must use approved lifecycle status values.
- Appointment status changes must be recorded for audit purposes.
--------------------------------------------------------------------- H

Patient Data Protection
- Patient data must be protected from unauthorized access.
- Access to protected patient information must follow approved data-protection controls.
--------------------------------------------------------------------- J

Appointment History Details
- Patients can review detailed history for previous appointments.
- Appointment history details can be displayed with the corresponding appointment record.
